Room 5: The Real Culprit

Is this the end?

"If I am ever dead, it was Stacy and Wilson," You read aloud after having discovered Mary's hidden message. Those were the names of two of the suspects, so you decide to have your agent start looking into the sibling pair.

This was an amazing piece of new evidence. But how could Mary be so sure about Stacy and Wilson killing her, especially since they were siblings? It just didn't make sense to you. However, with the altered will that newly listed Stacy and Wilson as beneficiares, you had just the right amount of evidence to issue a search warrant for their hotel room.

After a day, your agent is back with information. "Turns out, Mary was adopted," Your agent spoke. "I'm guessing that if it really was Stacy and Wilson, they must've done it out of jealousy."

"At their age?" You scoff. With Mary being almost 60, Stacy and Wilson weren't exactly young either.

Just then, the team handling the search through Stacy and Wilson's hotel room reported back to you. "We didn't find anything too special," they state. "But you might find this interesting. We haven't been able to crack the code to get into this notebook."

One of them hands you a worn brown notebook, with the word "Mary" printed on the front in bright gold calligraphy. It was fastened with a stubborn four-digit code lock. "This could be it," You exclaim. "This notebook probably contains all the secrets and Mary's inner thoughts, which might help us figure out her relationships and crack the case!"

MARY

CODE: _ _ _ _

  • Some numbers love their eyes. Who are they?

WHAT IS THE CODE TO MARY'S DIARY?

Note: Rules from the Puzzle Rulebook continue to apply.

The lock clicks open.

You flip through the diary, finding years upon years of diary entries. The book is thick - it just might contain Mary's entire life story.

December 15th, 1977 - I just got adopted today. Finally leaving behind this hell of an orphanage, but I don't know if this new life is going to be any better. At least they're rich, it seems? I won't go hungry anymore?

You keep flipping.

March 3rd, 1978 - It's been around four months since I got here. Yesterday my siblings Stacy and Wilson got to the house. I've been spending quite a bit of time with old Ms. Johnson. She does seem to quite like puzzles.

March 12th, 1978 - Stacy and Wilson hate me. I don't know what it was I ever did to them. Although I have noticed that Ms. Johnson doesn't show them much affection. Isn't that strange?

May 2nd, 1982 - It's been almost five years since I first came to the Johnson mansion. I have to say, I've grown to quite like Mr. and Ms. Johnson. Especially Ms. Johnson. I have always loved her puzzles, and I love creating puzzles for her as well.

Flipping past years of entries, something catches your eye.

April 22nd, 1987 - I did not expect my first day back of college to be filled with such tragedy and misery. I mourn the loss of Mr. and Ms. Johnson, who were like the parents I never had.

May 6th, 1987 - I was named the main beneficiary??? What the duck?

July 2nd, 1987 - I've had to go into hiding against Stacy and Wilson. They've lost everything, whereas I have everything they ever wanted.

December 4th, 1987 - Shitake mushrooms - they're hunting me down. I don't know what to do.

July 2nd, 1987 - I've had to go into hiding against Stacy and Wilson. They've lost everything, whereas I have everything they ever wanted.

You quickly move past the pages of Mary describing her flight to America from Italy, and her moving to Nashville.

July 29th, 1993 - Today I met someone very kind - Was it John? His name? He's quite the looker too.

September 24th, 1993 - I feel like I keep seeing John around nowadays, almost like I want to see him. I don't like how I might be falling for him.

September 27th, 1993 - HE CONFESSED. OH MY GOD. I OFFICIALLY HAVE A BOYFRIEND!!

You smile as Mary proceeded to describe her happy relationship days with John.

September 27th, 1998 - MARRIED TODAY! And on the same day as when he first confessed to me too! I love John so so much - we're going to stay together forever!

March 30th, 1999 - We officially handed over all the money to Obsidian Financials - my best friend's bank. I'm ready to close that chapter of my life and live as any normal married woman, one without $1.134 billion dollars to her name, and two crazy money-hungry siblings hunting her down.

Years passed without much else - Mary occasionally wrote about her happy married life, her workplace.

November 17th, 2006 - Oh no. I think Stacy and Wilson know I'm in the U.S. Do I need to move again?

January 19th, 2007 - I think it's okay, I've even changed my last name and everything, I look nothing like the young teenager they'd last seen. How could they ever find me in such a vast country?

January 23rd, 2009 - If I ever die, I want the world to know what terrible people Stacy and Wilson are.

March 12th, 2014 - I'm going to leave all the money to Karen. John knows - he understands why I wouldn't want him in possession of the money. I know my sweet best friend Karen will know exactly how to handle the money. Maybe I should start researching charities?

You skip through more years of scarce journal entries, with just a couple updates here and there.

August 24th, 2023 - Duck. I think Stacy and Wilson have figured out I'm in Tennessee. Duck, duck, duck. But I'm so old now and they're so old now, how are they still trying to hunt me down?

July 16th, 2024 - I'd forgotten how amazing of a hacker Stacy was. Has she found my address? It's possible she's gotten access to Obsidian Financials... oh what do I do?

February 3rd, 2025 - How could John do something like that to me? I trusted him... I really did...

February 4th, 2025 - I could not even process this fact yesterday, but today I can. I've been acting normal around John, but really, I know everything.

February 5th, 2025 - It's crazy. Years and years of calls and messages with Wilson on a secret spare phone that even I didn't know about. He's been plotting with them to murder me.

March 11th, 2025 - Guess they still haven't striked. I've decided to accept my fate. Once I die, my secrets will finally be revealed to the world.

You sit back, still reeling with all your new discoveries. Mary's diary proves it - John, Stacy, and Wilson had all been conspiring against her. Poor Mary - everyone she could've trusted, everyone she loved, either died or turned out to be terrible people.

You understand now that it was your duty to serve Mary justice.

This diary contained everything, all the evidence you needed to arrest all three of them - John, Stacy, and Wilson. With the overwhelming evidence against them, it didn't take long for them to finally be incarcerated, and for Mary's justice to finally be served.

CASE CLOSED. THE END.
