Room 1: The First Encounter

This where the story all began...

You are a world-class detective who is known for cracking even the crime scene's most difficult mysteries. Today, a distressed stranger enters your office in a burst of panic.

"My wife! My wife!" He screams in an intense fit of desperation. "Please help me, detective , please! I found her dead inside her room, with a knife stabbed straight through her heart! Oh detective, please help me! I've already reported the incident to the police, and they're currently investigating the murder site... but no luck so far... so I've come to you as my last hope."

"Calm down, calm down," You say. "I'm here to help. Please tell me your name?"

The stranger replies, "John, detective."

"And your wife's name?"

"Mary," says the stranger.

A couple days later...

The forensics team handling the investigation in Mary's room have started to uncover evidence. They found a series of newspapers scattered around the room.

You take a look at the newspapers. It seems the culprit is playing a game with both you and the authorities - one which you are starting to unravel.

The investigation has also led to the discovery of a puzzle rulebook left behind by the culprit. The police have handed it to you. For now, it seems the culprit is not aware that you are in posession of this rulebook.

THE PUZZLE RULEBOOK

  • Answers may contain words or contain numbers, but will never include both words and numbers.
  • Answers with words will be entirely lowercase.
  • Answers with words could have more than one word (eg. phrases, full names, etc.)
  • Answers with words may contain spaces in between the two (or more) words, but also may not.
  • Answers with numbers will not contain spaces.
  • Answers will never contain special symbols, regardless of words or numbers.
